Rules & Guidelines

Challenge Rules & Guidelines

Fair play, timing policies, and monthly score resets

1 User Authorization Policy

This is a private group challenge (up to 5 participants). New members can view the dashboard immediately upon registering, but must be authorized by Admin before logging workouts or checkmarks.

2 3-Day Backdating Window

To encourage accountability, you may only log entries for today or up to 3 days in the past (Today, -1 day, -2 days, -3 days). Submissions older than 3 days are locked and require Admin authorization override.

3 Scoring System

  • Habit Checklist: 1 point per checkbox per day (Max 3 points/day).
  • Exercise Time: Exact total hours and minutes accumulated across all logged sessions.

4 Monthly Challenge Reset

Leaderboards refresh automatically each month while maintaining previous cycle data.
